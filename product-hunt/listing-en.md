## Description

Itsees is a local-first desktop travel companion. Pick a route and let your tiny traveler wander while you focus. A four-hour journey gradually reveals maps, postcards, and destination keepsakes. The free Apple Silicon beta includes 15 storybook routes, 15 real-world places, weather-aware visuals, music, and a bilingual travel journal.

## Maker first comment

Hi Product Hunt — we built Itsees around a small idea: progress does not always need to look like another dashboard.

Your tiny desktop companion travels while you work. Choose a route, pack a small bag, and let the journey unfold over four hours. Along the way, Itsees reveals map segments and brings home illustrated postcards and destination-specific keepsakes. Your travel state and collection stay on your computer, and the companion can collapse into a quiet desktop pet whenever you need more space.

Beta 6 is available for Apple Silicon Macs in English and Simplified Chinese. It includes 15 storybook routes and 15 real-world places, a local travel journal, weather-aware visuals with a local fallback, and background music. This update adds a live in-progress album, paginated souvenirs and travel history, a clearer timeline, consistent keepsake cards, more concise keepsake descriptions, and a reliable one-click travel-record reset.

Somewhere out there, a wide world is waiting. Let it wander ahead — and bring the wonder home.

We would especially value feedback on whether the four-hour rhythm feels calming, motivating, or too slow — and where you would send your tiny traveler next. Thank you for taking a look.
