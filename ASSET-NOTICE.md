# Itsees Asset Notice

Confirmed by Lucy Zhang on 2026-08-01.

The visual and audio assets distributed with Itsees are original project assets authored by Lucy Zhang or generated through accounts she controls. The release does not directly include downloaded web photographs, stock-library media, third-party character intellectual property, or third-party music.

## Asset groups

- **Core:** Phase 1 themes, maps, pets, travel-pack items, souvenirs, and their runtime derivatives were authored by Lucy Zhang or generated through accounts she controls.
- **Atlas:** The distributed landmark and image-world artwork is original project-generated illustration. Wikimedia and Wikipedia materials were used only for landmark research; the reference photographs are excluded from the application and release packages.
- **Music:** The 210 weather background tracks were generated through an account controlled by Lucy Zhang using ChatCut. No third-party audio files are directly included. ChatCut describes generated music as original, royalty-free, and safe to publish in its [AI Music documentation](https://chatcut.io/features/ai-music), reviewed on 2026-08-01.

The file-level paths, byte sizes, and SHA-256 values are recorded separately in the release asset manifest. This notice records the project owner's source and redistribution confirmation; it is not a third-party legal opinion.
