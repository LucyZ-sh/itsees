# Itsees 0.1.0-beta.6

## Highlights

- Removes the duplicate travel settings and collection block from the home page.
- Shows the current journey in the backpack album before its postcards arrive.
- Adds numbered pagination to albums, souvenirs, and travel history.
- Presents travel history as a clearer timeline with dates below its markers.
- Keeps souvenir cards equal in height and removes repetitive card descriptions.
- Shortens full-screen souvenir descriptions to the material and texture note.
- Fixes desktop travel-record reset so the first confirmation succeeds.
- Removes the Codex Skin chapter from the official website.
