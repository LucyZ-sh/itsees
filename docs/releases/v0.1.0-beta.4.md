# Itsees 0.1.0-beta.4

Somewhere out there, a wide world is waiting. Let it wander ahead — and bring the wonder home.

## What changed

- Added a native English launch animation while keeping Chinese as the first-run default.
- Remembered the selected language so later launches use the matching animation.
- Updated the official website's English product promise and Beta 4 download metadata.
- Restored keyboard and screen-reader focus after the launch animation finishes.
- Moved the Phase 1 completion badge out of CSS-generated content and removed redundant autofocus markup.
- Included the resilient destination music delivery and Phase 2 handoff improvements shipped since the first public beta.
